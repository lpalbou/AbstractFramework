Your first hour

Install, try it, make it yours

Install with the right profile 15 min

The installer picks the profile for your machine: apple on an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14+, which brings the local MLX engines for images and video; gpu on Linux with an NVIDIA or AMD GPU; light elsewhere, where generation goes through a service.

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lpalbou/AbstractFramework/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

Let the console choose models that fit 10 min

In the first-run guide’s Default model step, Use recommended defaults sets text, voice, images and video wherever this computer can run them; a route it cannot run shows the reason. The Models tab has Image, Video and Voice capability filters, the download size of each build, and a fit badge. Downloads start only when you click Download.

Make your first image 10 min

Open the console’s Sandbox tab and generate an image against your defaults. From Python, the same thing is one call:

from abstractcore import create_llm
llm = create_llm("ollama", model="qwen3:4b")
image = llm.generate("A red ceramic mug on a white table.", output="image")
open("mug.png", "wb").write(image.outputs["image"][0].data)

Edits take a source image (and optional style references) with the same output="image". See AbstractVision.

Draw it as a workflow 15 min

Open Flow from the console’s Apps page (/apps/flow/) and chain an LLM step that writes a scene, an image step that paints it and a voice step that narrates it. One turn can return text, audio and an image, all stored with the run.

Add music and a voice 10 min

AbstractMusic writes music from a prompt: through ACE Music (set ACEMUSIC_API_KEY) or locally with ACE-Step. AbstractVoice clones a voice from a reference recording with OmniVoice, F5-TTS or Chroma locally, or an OpenAI-compatible service.

Then make it yours

TRY 1

A short video

On an Apple Silicon Mac with 96 GB or more, the recommended video route is Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B through MLX-Gen (on 64 GB, set it yourself and generate at 832×480). Ask for "a crystal clear river in the mountains", or animate a still with image-to-video.

A soundtrack

Good to know

Honest notes

Hardware, plainly. Local image and video models need a lot of memory. On NVIDIA with Diffusers, the AbstractVision guide matches GPUs with up to 16 GB of VRAM to SD 1.5 and 24–32 GB to FLUX.2 Klein 4B. For Wan 2.2 video through MLX-Gen, TI2V-5B needs about 63.5 GiB at its default 1280×704 size, so the console recommends it from 96 GB; at 832×480 it needs about 34.3 GiB, which a 64 GB Mac can run when you set the video route yourself. The larger A14B models need more: about 43.9 GiB at 640×352 and 75.2 GiB at their default size (needs include overhead, from runs on a 128 GB Mac). Which model fits which Mac. When your machine is too small, the console says so, and a service is the better route.
Licences travel with the model. Each engine keeps its own terms: Supertonic voices, Piper voices and Stable Audio have their own licences, MusicGen is for non-commercial use, and some 3D and audio models are gated. The package pages list them.
Only clone voices you are allowed to use. Voice cloning works from a short reference recording; use your own voice or one you have permission for.
