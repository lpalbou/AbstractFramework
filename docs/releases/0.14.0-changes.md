# AbstractFramework 0.14.0: what changes for you

> Status: **draft, 2026-11-10, about 10:00 Paris**, written for your test of the candidate. Most packages of the wave
> are on their registries already; the gateway 0.21.0, the five apps and the root 0.14.0 are not. Until root 0.14.0
> is published, `pip install abstractframework` and the one-line installers still give you 0.13.0. Exact versions:
> the [appendix](#6-appendix-versions-tags-and-tests) and [0.14.0-pins-draft.md](0.14.0-pins-draft.md).

## 1. What this release is

- Entities get a clear life: five states, own time only when you give it, a Stop that really stops, visits that never fail.
- Every memory gets a title and a summary written by a model when it forms, and sleep repairs the older ones.
- Sleep groups an entity's memories into topics, which the entity can browse and the Entity app draws.
- You can bring any entity into a conversation with `@` from Code, the Assistant and the Entity app, and see who answered.
- Voice and vision are proven on Linux (NVIDIA and processor-only) with one-line installers; agents get 50 steps by default.

## 2. What you will notice

### An entity's life cycle

- **Five states, one at a time: idle, visit, work, own time, sleep.** The Entity app's phase bar shows all five. A new
  entity starts idle: awake, visitable, with no own time until you give it.
- **Own time is yours to give.** The personal toggle is the only switch. Nothing starts own time by itself: a visit, a
  sleep or a job that interrupted it hands it back only while your grant is still on.
- **No work switch.** Work starts when a job is queued for the entity (a work order or a task) and the bar says
  "2 jobs queued". When the jobs are done it goes back to its own time if granted, otherwise to idle.
- **Stop and Resume.** The red Stop halts everything at once, takes own time back and lists what it stopped (Stop again
  retries what is left). Resume brings the entity back idle and restores nothing. Tested live: a Stop 1.7 s into a
  reply cancelled the model call before it started, and no memory was written after it.
- **A visit never fails.** Opening a visit waits as long as it takes (it used to give up after 55 s with "retry
  shortly"); an entity busy with its own time stops after the step in progress and lets you in. A model call that
  fails ends that one turn ("This turn could not be answered: …"), never the visit. "Stopped" is the only refusal.
- **A gateway restart resumes own time.** A restart is not a Stop: entities whose own time was granted get it back at
  start (before, each needed a manual Retry), after any visit, sleep or job in progress. Never for a stopped entity.
- **Sleep has reasons.** An idle entity sleeps for maintenance by itself (thresholds under Topics) and the line says
  why ("asleep: 24 h since the last sleep"). It wakes when the maintenance is done; a visit interrupts it.

### Memories: titles and summaries

- **Every memory is titled and summarised when it forms**: visit moments and closing summaries, lessons, interests,
  questions, public diary entries, a newborn's traits. Titles are in sentence case; summaries name the entity, never
  "the agent" or "the assistant".
- **Nothing pretends.** While a title is still being written, the apps say "title being written…", never a
  placeholder or words cut from the body. The request waits in the entity's own queue and is retried in idle time.
- **Sleep repairs the old ones.** Older memories with mechanical titles ("session reflection: …", "trait-0",
  "Dream: …") or cut summaries are rewritten in place during sleep. Every earlier version is kept.
- **Nothing is cut when saved any more**; screens shorten for display only. An older record whose summary was cut
  shows its title and "Older record: its summary was cut when saved; he rewrites it in his sleep." instead.
- **What a reply kept shows as badges** under its words (diary entry, lesson, interest, question, realization) instead
  of lines like "[kept in diary - …]". A private entry reads "private diary entry" with no words; one formed at the
  visit's close reads "forming…". Click to read it whole. In the Entity app and the Assistant web app.

### Topics

- **What they are.** A topic is a group of memories about one subject, with its own written title and summary. Each
  memory belongs to one.
- **How they form.** In sleep, after the repair, memory groups everything by meaning, keeps each topic's identity from
  night to night and names the new or changed ones (a quarter of the night's writing budget is kept for that). A
  memory formed by day joins its nearest topic provisionally. Until the first sleep the graph has no circles.
- **The Topics panel and the graph.** In the Entity app the topics are the graph's layout, one circle each. The panel
  lists them biggest first; unfold one for its dates, its use this week, what last night changed and its bridges to
  other topics. Circles can be dragged; the ledger says "His topics changed". 895 memories load in 2 s, then 5 ms.
- **The `topics` tool.** The entity can list its topics, open one, ask which topic a memory belongs to, or search by
  meaning, so it knows what it knows and can dig into part of its memory.
- **When it sleeps.** An idle entity sleeps after 24 hours since its last full sleep, after 500 new memories, or, new,
  when more than 15 % of its memories are not yet whole (only provisionally placed, without a search vector, or with
  a title or summary still being written).
- **Settings → Mind**: the model that writes summaries and the one that understands and dreams ("Same as the mind" or
  their own), memories per turn (1 to 400, default 50), how readily it rethinks what it understood, and under
  **Sleep** the hours and new-memory thresholds; each field saves itself. The 15 % share has no field yet.

### Talking to entities from any app

- **`@` an entity** in Code web, the Assistant web app, the Entity app (visits and flow-brain conversations) and the
  Code terminal. A pick becomes a chip and warns once that the entity "will read this conversation's history". The
  conversation's agent answers only its part, or stays silent, then each entity answers in turn.
- **Who answered.** Every reply names its author: the agent's workflow name with an "agent" tag, or the entity's
  name and orb with an "entity" tag. A reload or another device shows the same.
- **Nothing is asked twice.** An entity busy in another conversation is skipped with one sentence. If one cannot
  answer, your words go back in the box with only the unanswered chips; an answer lost on the way is returned.

### Agents and Code

- **50 steps by default, everywhere** (was 20): agents, new Flow Agent nodes, entity visits and the gateway. A fresh
  install stores 50. Code web has one "Iteration limit" field under Model → Limits & streaming ("Gateway default
  (50)"). A sub-agent never gets more steps than its parent.
- **Dev servers outlive the turn.** A server an agent starts in Code keeps running for the next turn; it stops when the
  agent or you stop it, when the conversation is archived, or when the gateway stops.
- **Tool results are kept whole.** Everything a tool returns during an entity's activity (a page, a whole file, a
  command's output, an image) is stored in the entity's home and linked from its turn, so nothing it saw is lost.
- **Session kinds.** Conversations, docs, notes, automation runs and the framework's own processes are told apart, so
  automation runs leave your conversation list.
- Also: the Code terminal shows an automation as panels, the Observer's Board is redesigned, and an image attached in
  a visit reaches the entity's model as an image.

### Settings, skills and the gateway

- **Core never runs on defaults.** If AbstractCore's settings file cannot be read, Core stops and names the file and
  the error; the gateway answers with the same and keeps serving the rest. Before, the file was renamed and Core ran
  silently on defaults (this Mac had 187 such renamed copies).
- **Skills are always reachable.** An activated skill can always be read by the agent, even inside a folder the
  workspace's ignore rules hide. New installs register the `abstractcore` and `abstractframework-gateway` skills.
- **Keep the Mac awake** is now a setting (on by default) in the web console's Gateway card and the tray's Settings
  menu, applied at once.
- Also: HTTPS on your Tailscale name while the gateway runs; a provider key goes only to the address it was saved
  for; a model endpoint that is off or asleep makes a run wait for it instead of failing.

### Voice and vision

- **Voice on Linux.** `abstractvoice[gpu]` installs on a clean Ubuntu server; on NVIDIA, Supertonic and Piper run on
  the card (8 s of speech: 0.09 s warm, 1.6 s on the processor) and Qwen3-TTS speaks faster than real time. Proven on
  an Ubuntu 24.04 NVIDIA host and an Ubuntu 26.04 processor-only host.
- **Vision on Linux.** Proven on the same two hosts: FLUX.2 klein 4B makes a 1024² image in 18-20 s on a 16 GB card,
  and the processor path works. Fixed: 8-bit GGUF presets downloaded every quantization of a model (258 GB for a 22 GB
  file) and now fetch only theirs; Diffusers downloads take only what loads (SDXL-turbo 41.6 GB → 6.9 GB); a GPU out
  of memory says what to change; GGUF runs work again on Ubuntu 24.04 and use the model's own steps and guidance.
- **Read aloud repaired.** Markdown is read as words (no code blocks, no symbols), a voice that misses its end stops
  where it went quiet instead of running up to 164 s, a cloned voice is no longer dropped for scoring slightly low,
  and turning Read aloud off really stops it, on phones and in Safari too.

### The apps' shared parts

- **Reconnects.** A conversation comes back by itself after the app server or the gateway restarts and shows what
  happened meanwhile. Not reaching the gateway is one notice with **Retry now** and **Stop retrying**; the run status
  keeps saying what the gateway last said.
- **Avatars.** Seven ready designs are offered (default `watch-halo`); retired designs keep rendering as a ready one.

## 3. What changed under the hood

- **The Stop fence.** Each entity home has a stop counter that Stop raises first. All work carries the number it was
  admitted with and checks it before each write; older work is refused and recorded "stopped by the operator", never
  as a failure. **What a Stop guarantees:** in the gateway and the own-time process, no new model call, tool, memory
  or diary write, stored result, title writing or maintenance step starts after it; Resume lands idle.
- **Four known gaps of the gateway's Stop** (backlog 1193, first in the next wave):
  1. A memory or diary write already running when you Stop a summoned entity, or one made through the operator's own
     routes, can still finish: Stop cancels the run but cannot pull back a write in progress.
  2. A mention joined into another entity's visit runs to its end (on purpose: visits never fail); a Stop of the
     visited entity in the instant that turn is saved can be overwritten by it.
  3. A visit opened before the upgrade carries no stop number and is not fully covered: close open visits before you
     upgrade.
  4. Requests waiting in line when you Stop are labelled "failed"; they should read "stopped".
- **The write barrier.** At the moment of the Stop, a memory write that already passed its check finishes first, and
  every later one sees the Stop, so no write lands half before and half after. One small window is left: the search
  index can take at most one vector write per writer just after the Stop.
- **The written-metadata contract.** Memory itself calls no model: the entity's summaries model writes every title and
  summary, or the field is honestly marked pending with a task in the home's queue. Each rewrite keeps the earlier
  versions with who wrote them and why. The briefing names the entity and asks for sentence case.
- **Topics, one owner per layer.** Memory forms, places and stores the topics (one version per sleep, never mixed
  into recall); the runtime runs that step in the entity's sleep and offers the `topics` tool; the gateway serves the
  topics, the memory graph and the change notice; the Entity app only draws. Nothing is computed when you open a page.

## 4. Known limits and what comes next

- **Old diary titles take time.** A few hundred older diary entries of a long-lived entity need about ten nights at
  the default budget (64 writing calls per sleep); raising "rebuilds per turn" in Settings → Mind does more by day.
- **Unchanged topics keep their old wording** until their memories change; a topic left untitled on a busy night can
  wait behind the repair queue (fixed in AbstractMemory 0.5.1, running on this Mac, not in a clean 0.14.0 install).
- **Records marked "cut" by mistake**: reflections formed on this Mac with pre-release builds read as cut until sleep
  rewrites them.
- **Shipped agent bundles still carry 20 steps** (1191). An install that never stored the setting:
  `abstractgateway config set agents.max_iterations 50`. A sub-workflow can also exceed your entity step ceiling.
- **Next wave, filed:** a mention becomes a job when the entity judges it is one, and it reports back (1187); the
  entity can look up what happened at a given time, and its own time runs as real runtime runs (1188); `@` names
  stay inside your text as badges, and each entity gets its own part of the message (1189); the graph's usage colours
  count the whole life, not the loaded window (1190); the release checks' two blind spots (1192).
- **Waiting for your view:** should sleep fold or prune memories that carry no information (1185)? Private means
  private from others, not from you (1186): nothing is built before you rediscuss it.
- **Decisions for you before the cut:**
  1. The conversation's agent sometimes still answers a message meant for an entity (small models). Proposal: when a
     message has mentions, the agent runs only if you also pick it. Recommended: yes.
  2. A one-line topics reminder at the start of each entity turn ("you have N topics; closest …"). Recommended: no.
  3. Code web as 0.16.0 or 0.15.21 (recommended 0.16.0); Assistant web in the root's app list (recommended: yes).
- Smaller: dev servers in split mode stop with the runner, not the conversation; the terminal console has no
  keep-awake row yet; the Windows voice installer has not run on a Windows machine yet.

## 5. How to install and upgrade

Once root 0.14.0 is published:

- **The three profiles**: `pip install abstractframework` (cloud and endpoint servers), `"abstractframework[apple]"`
  (Apple Silicon, local MLX), `"abstractframework[gpu]"` (NVIDIA or ROCm). One line installs or upgrades everything:
  `curl -LsSf https://abstractframework.ai/install.sh | sh` (Windows:
  `powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://abstractframework.ai/install.ps1 | iex"`), or
  `abstractgateway update --check` then `abstractgateway update` from an installed gateway.
- **One-line installers for one package** (no admin rights, no Python needed; Core and Voice leave a framework install
  alone). They go live with the website update after the root release (today they answer 404):
  - Core: `curl -LsSf https://abstractframework.ai/core/install.sh | sh`
  - Voice: `curl -LsSf https://abstractframework.ai/voice/install.sh | sh`
  - Vision: `curl -LsSf https://abstractframework.ai/vision/install.sh | sh`
  - Windows: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://abstractframework.ai/<core|voice|vision>/install.ps1 | iex"`
- **GPU voices on NVIDIA**: with the gpu profile on Linux or Windows, driver 580 or newer and a card of compute
  capability 7.5 or newer, the installer adds ONNX Runtime's CUDA build by itself; the summary's "GPU voices:" line
  says if it is in place. By hand: `pip install "abstractvoice[onnx-cuda]==0.21.0"`, then, if the voices still run on
  the processor, `pip install --force-reinstall --no-deps onnxruntime-gpu`.

**For anyone building on the gateway or embedding the runtime:**

- **Mentions**: send the picked entities as `mentions` on `POST /runs/start`, then ask each one with
  `POST /sessions/{session_id}/mentions` (`{entity, text, mention_index}`). Never parse names from the text. Show
  `author` on each turn and `notices` as badges (replies no longer contain bracket markers).
- **Life cycle**: read `GET /entities/{name}/life_state`; the only toggle is `personal` (`work` answers 422); Stop,
  Resume and Retry are `POST /entities/{name}/lifecycle`. The old state and loop routes still work and answer
  `Deprecation: true`. Visit doors now wait without limit: allow long waits (`loop_busy` is gone).
- **Topics**: `GET /entities/{name}/communities` serves payload version 2 (no stand-in before the first sleep),
  `GET /entities/{name}/graph` the whole graph, and the replay stream a `communities_changed` notice.
- **Runtime embedders**: every entity state write takes a `fence=` with no default (`open_home`,
  `open_entity_runtime`, `ChatSession`, `spawn_loop_process`); `ChatSession.fence` replaces `cancel_check`; the
  authoring calls need `entity_name=`. AbstractMemory 0.5.0 homes that form memories need
  `MemorySystem(..., task_queue=True)` and an `author` callable, and a title may be `None` while pending.
- **Libraries**: AbstractTUI 0.8.0 (a press outside a modal closes it; `ChatMessage.authored_by`; upgrade its three
  crates together); AbstractAvatar 0.2.0 (store design ids, not catalog numbers; offer only the ready designs);
  AbstractVoice 0.21.0 (the clone speaker check is a setting, the two environment variables are gone).
