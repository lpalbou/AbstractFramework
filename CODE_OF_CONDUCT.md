# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in AbstractFramework — maintainers, contributors, reporters — is expected
to act professionally and treat others with respect.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Focus critique on code and ideas, never on people.
- Assume good faith; disagree with evidence and specifics.
- Respect maintainers' time: clear reports, minimal reproductions.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or sustained off-topic argument.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to contact@abstractframework.ai. Reports are reviewed confidentially;
consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from project spaces, at the maintainers'
discretion.
